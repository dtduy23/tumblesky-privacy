Tumble Sky — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Tumble Sky ("the game") uses. Questions and requests: duyd4010@gmail.com

Data saved on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your device: best scores, floors completed, coins and diamonds, purchase records, unlocked and selected items, achievements, tutorial state, sound, haptics, control and graphics settings. This data stays on your device and is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it, and the backup behavior depends on your operating system.

The developer does not operate a server that receives your data, and the game has no account registration.

Advertising

The game shows a banner ad along the bottom of the screen, a full-screen ad between runs now and then (after several runs or several minutes of play, never during a run), and optional rewarded video ads, all served by Google AdMob. A rewarded ad plays only when you choose to watch one. The NO ADS purchase removes banners and full-screen ads.

AdMob may collect device and usage data, which can include your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information, and ad interactions. Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalize ads. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before requesting ads. You can change your choice from the PRIVACY button on the About screen when that button is shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases

Coins and diamonds are sold through Google Play. Google processes your payment, and the developer does not receive your card or other payment details. The game stores an order reference on your device so that a purchase is delivered once. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Device features

Optional haptics use your device's vibration. The game does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
